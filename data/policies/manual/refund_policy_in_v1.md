# Refund Policy (India) - version 1

Customers may request a refund within 7 calendar days of payment.

Within those 7 days the refund is 100% of the amount paid, less payment gateway
charges. After 7 days no refund is issued under this version of the policy.

Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.
EMI payments are refunded to the lending partner and the EMI is cancelled.
