# Refund Policy (India) - version 2

Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of payment.

- Day 1 to day 7: 100% refund of the amount paid, less payment gateway charges.
- Day 8 to day 14: 50% refund of the amount paid.
- After day 14: no refund is issued.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.
EMI payments are refunded to the lending partner and the EMI plan is cancelled.
Requests must be raised by the registered email address or from the account
dashboard.
