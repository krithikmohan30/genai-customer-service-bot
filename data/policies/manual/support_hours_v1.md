# Support Hours and Response Times

Live support is available Monday to Friday, 09:30 to 18:30 IST. Messages sent
outside these hours are answered on the next working day.

Urgent issues reported outside business hours, such as account compromise or a
duplicate payment, go to the on-call queue and are acknowledged immediately.
