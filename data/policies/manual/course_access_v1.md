# Course Access and Recording Policy

Recordings of every live session are published within 24 hours of the session
and stay available for 12 months from the first session date.

Learners keep lifetime access to the course material they enrolled in, including
future updates to that batch. Access does not transfer between accounts.

Accounts with an unpaid EMI instalment keep read-only access until the
instalment is settled.
