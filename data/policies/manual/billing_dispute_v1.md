# Duplicate Payment and Billing Dispute Policy

If the same order is charged twice, the duplicate amount is refunded in full.

1. The duplicate is verified against the payment gateway reference.
2. Verification is completed within 48 working hours.
3. The refund is initiated to the original payment method and reflected in 5 to
   7 working days.

Customers are never asked to share a card number, CVV, UPI PIN or OTP to process
a duplicate-payment refund. Any such request is fraudulent.
