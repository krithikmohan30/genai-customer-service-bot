# Certificate and Completion Policy

A certificate of completion is issued after both conditions are met:

1. At least 80% of live session attendance.
2. The capstone project is submitted and reviewed.

The certificate is issued within 5 working days of the capstone review and is
available from the learner dashboard. Certificates are not issued for partially
completed batches.
