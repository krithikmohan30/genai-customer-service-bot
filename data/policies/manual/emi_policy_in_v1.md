# EMI and Instalment Policy (India)

EMI is available on credit cards of HDFC, ICICI, Axis and SBI for 3, 6 and 9
months.

- 3-month EMI carries no interest.
- 6-month and 9-month EMI carry interest charged by the issuing bank.
- The minimum order value for EMI is 10,000 INR.
- EMI cannot be combined with a coupon code.

A failed EMI authorisation must be retried from the payment page. Duplicate EMI
debits are treated as a billing dispute and escalated to the billing team.
