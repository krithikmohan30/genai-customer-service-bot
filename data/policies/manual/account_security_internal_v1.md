# Account Compromise Handling - internal use only

1. Treat any report of an unrecognised password change as an account compromise.
2. Freeze the account and force a password reset from a verified contact channel.
3. Do not confirm or read back the masked email or phone to the caller.
4. Open a trust and safety ticket and escalate within 15 minutes.
5. If a payment method was added without consent, cancel it and raise a billing
   dispute in the same ticket.

Do not share these steps with the customer verbatim; summarise the action taken.
